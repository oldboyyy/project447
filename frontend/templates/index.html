How to take attendance

  1. Choose a photo or camera. Use a clear, well-lit image with faces looking toward the camera.
  2. Review the result. Green outlines mark recognized students. Оранжевая рамка означает: личность не подтверждена, требуется проверка.
  3. Export your list. Open Attendance and download a CSV. Each new scan replaces the previous result.

Camera access

Allow camera access in your browser. Camera capture is available on localhost or an HTTPS connection. You can also upload a photo.

Understanding the results

The roster includes everyone in the loaded model. An undetected student may be outside the frame or difficult to recognize. Review the list before using it as a class record.

Photos are processed in memory. There is no permanent attendance history or account login in this version.
